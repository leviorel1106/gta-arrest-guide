Antes de empezar

Tres reglas que lo hicieron funcionar.

1

El sentimiento son 13 años

La gente no llora por un juego. Llora por quién era cuando empezó a jugar. Todas las películas de esta tendencia que pegaron hablan del paso del tiempo, sin un solo diálogo.

2

Un giro en el final

Todos hicieron la despedida. Aquí la policía por fin gana después de 13 años, y el remate es un abrazo y una medalla en vez de esposas. Una sorpresa clara, nada que explicar.

3

Un lugar, una toma

Escribe la escena segundo a segundo, con bloques fijos para el look, el elenco, el lugar y las reglas. Seedance la filma en una sola toma, así la calle y las caras nunca cambian entre cortes.

Convierte a los personajes en actores reales

Sube una captura de cada personaje del juego y pide una foto real del mismo hombre, como si hubieran elegido a un actor para la película. Describe la cara, el pelo y la ropa que quieres mantener, y dile que quite todo rastro del juego.

Personaje del juego → actor real
Image 1 shows Michael De Santa, the video game character from GTA V. Create a genuine photograph of him as a REAL living man, as if a real actor were cast for a live-action film, instantly recognisable as Michael: late 40s, short dark hair combed back with grey at the temples, heavy brow, tired knowing eyes, strong jaw, slight double chin. Wardrobe: unbuttoned navy suit jacket over an open-collared white dress shirt, no tie. Expression: a warm, fatherly half-smile. Remove every trace of the game model: no polygon planes, no painted texture, no plastic skin, no CGI. Real pores, real eyelids, real hairline, slight skin imperfections, subtle asymmetry, unretouched skin. Half-body portrait, 3:4, standing on a dry canyon road in the Los Angeles hills at golden-hour sunset, warm low backlight from the right, shot on ARRI Alexa, 50mm, f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400 look, natural colors, soft bokeh. Photoreal, no text, no logos.
Tip: que caigan bien. Mi primer Trevor parecía el villano de una película de terror. "Adorable, un poco loco, nunca amenazante" lo arregló.
2GPT IMAGE · EN KOLBO

Fija cada cara con una hoja de personaje

Usa la foto que elegiste como referencia y pide una hoja de la misma persona desde varios ángulos. Esta hoja es lo que lee el modelo de video, y es lo que mantiene la misma cara en cada plano.

Prompt de hoja de personaje
Character reference sheet of the SAME real person shown in the reference image(s): keep the face, age, hair, skin and wardrobe strictly identical. Plain light grey studio background, even soft light, photographic, real person not a 3D render. Top row: 4 full-body views (front, three-quarter left, side profile, back). Bottom row: 4 head-and-shoulders views (front, three-quarter left, profile, three-quarter right) with a neutral expression. Real skin texture, pores, subtle asymmetry. No text, no labels, no numbers, no logos. Keep his warm, lovable look, not menacing. Wardrobe: off-white V-neck t-shirt with light road dust, worn blue jeans, brown work boots; the small dotted CUT HERE tattoo on the neck.

Haz lo mismo con cualquier personaje nuevo de tu historia. Aquí es el jefe de policía que entrega las medallas.

Un solo lugar vacío

Una imagen del lugar sin nadie: el bulevar de noche, las patrullas en círculo, la luz del helicóptero. Es todo lo que necesitas como referencia. Los policías, los ciudadanos, los uniformes y las medallas son palabras en el prompt, no más imágenes.

Prompt del lugar
Empty location photograph, no people. A wide six-lane city boulevard at night in downtown Los Angeles, palm trees, closed shops and a gas station on the side, more than twenty black-and-white police cruisers parked at angles across the road forming a wide ring around an empty patch of asphalt in the center, all light bars flashing red and blue, doors open, a helicopter above casting a white spotlight cone onto the empty center. Night in a big Los Angeles-style city, wet asphalt reflecting red and blue police lights, sodium street lamps, light haze. Low wide angle from the road surface, 24mm. Look: a frame from a big-budget Hollywood crime feature, shot on ARRI Alexa 65, fine 35mm film grain, natural colors, real textures, no retouching. No logos, no watermark.

Filma toda la película en una sola toma

Una toma de Seedance 2.5, 30 segundos, 9:16, con las hojas y el lugar como referencias. El prompt está escrito como habla un director:

  • Primero el gancho: puntos láser rojos en la cara de Trevor en el primer segundo.
  • Segundo a segundo: cada plano lleva su tamaño, ángulo, movimiento de cámara y una sola acción simple.
  • Un arco emocional del miedo a la sorpresa y a un abrazo grupal, así las caras nunca se reinician entre cortes.
The Last Arrest · el prompt real (9:16, 30s)
Three famous criminals kneel on a wet city boulevard at night, surrounded by the police, and instead of handcuffs the police chief gives them gold medals. Ultra-realistic live-action short film, a big-budget Hollywood crime film that turns into a warm comedy, 30 seconds, vertical 9:16, ten shots cut together inside one generation, shot on ARRI Alexa Mini with spherical primes, Cinestill 800T night colour, fine film grain. Use @Image1 as Michael, @Image2 as Franklin, @Image3 as Trevor and @Image4 as the police chief: each sheet is an identity reference only, never render a sheet, a grey studio background or more than one copy of anyone. @Image1 Michael: late 40s, short dark hair combed back with grey at the temples, heavy brow, tired knowing eyes; navy suit jacket open over a white open-collared shirt, dark trousers. @Image2 Franklin: about 25, slim, very short black hair, short neat beard; dark blue short-sleeve button shirt over a white long-sleeve tee, thin gold chain, dark jeans, white sneakers. @Image3 Trevor: about 45, balding with thin messy dark hair on the sides, weathered sunburnt skin, warm wild eyes, the small dotted CUT HERE tattoo around his neck; dirty off-white V-neck t-shirt, worn blue jeans, brown work boots; lovable, a little crazy, never menacing. @Image4 the chief: about 62, broad, short grey hair, thick grey moustache, deep forehead lines; dark navy formal chief uniform, four small silver stars on each collar, white shirt, black tie. Preserve each man's exact face, hair and clothes in every shot; do not transfer any trait of these four onto officers or extras. Use @Image5 as the environment: the same six-lane downtown boulevard, palm trees, the skyline ahead, a gas station on the right, more than twenty black-and-white police cruisers parked at angles in a wide ring, doors open, light bars flashing; the camera stays on this boulevard and invents no other street. The officers are about thirty men and women of mixed ages in dark navy long-sleeve uniforms, silver badges, black duty belts, a few in dark tactical vests, plain patches with no readable text. FIXED GEOGRAPHY: the three men kneel side by side on the wet asphalt in the empty centre of the ring, facing the skyline, Michael on the left, Trevor in the middle, Franklin on the right; the officers stand behind the open cruiser doors about fifteen metres away; the chief's cruiser is parked directly in front of the three men; a police helicopter hovers high above. LIGHTING: hard white helicopter spotlight from directly above as the key, pulsing red and blue light-bar strobes as the fill from all around, warm amber sodium street lamps as the rim from behind; the wet asphalt mirrors every colour. Palette: wet black, navy, police red, cobalt blue, sodium amber, the gold of the medals. CAMERA POSITIONS, reused: CAM A wide from behind the officers at the cruiser doors, shoulder height, 35mm, the three men small in the centre; CAM B low at ground level just behind the three men, looking toward the chief's cruiser, 35mm; CAM C front medium of the three men, chest up, 50mm; CAM D 85mm telephoto close-up on one face or on hands. Motion is subtle and real time: no slow motion, nobody runs, the camera only pushes in slowly or holds. Nobody speaks.

EMOTIONAL ARC: caught (0-2) → the tension of a real arrest (2-8) → dread (8-11) → shock (11-14) → disbelief (14-17) → warmth (17-21) → pride (21-24) → laughter that turns into tears (24-30). The expressions never jump backward and never reset between shots.
STYLE AND REALISM: unretouched skin with visible pores and rain on it, no beauty filter, no video-game look, no CGI look, real wet night. The only text anywhere is POLICE on the cruiser doors. No subtitles, no captions, no text overlays, no logos.
AUDIO: continuous night-city ambience: sirens that fade out after SHOT 3, the helicopter rotor, light rain, footsteps splashing, the clink of the medal, applause that grows from SHOT 6, laughter and a cheer at the end. No music, no speech.
AVOID: jitter, temporal flicker, facial drift between shots, clothing change, extra fingers, merged faces or bodies in the hug, a character sheet or white background in any frame, chaotic composition.
5DRAFT → FULL QUALITY

Primero el draft, luego Full Quality

Corre cada escena como draft (borrador) en 480p. Es rápido y barato, así puedes verla, arreglar el prompt y volver a correrla. Solo cuando la toma esté bien, mándala a Full Quality en Kolbo. Renderiza la misma toma otra vez en 1080p nativo.

Importante: los drafts que piensas terminar en Full Quality tienen que hacerse solo con imágenes de referencia, sin video de referencia.

Termínala en el idioma del propio juego

El último cuadro es lo que hace que la gente etiquete a sus amigos. Usé las pantallas reales de GTA V: cinco estrellas de búsqueda que parpadean y se apagan, y el "mission passed" amarillo con su lista. No las dibujes de memoria, cualquiera que haya jugado va a ver que está mal. Le pedí a Claude que las reconstruyera a partir de capturas reales del juego.

Prompt para Claude (adjunta 2-3 capturas reales)
I attached real screenshots of the GTA V "mission passed" screen. Rebuild it as a transparent PNG overlay for my video, exactly like the game: trace the yellow "mission passed" lettering from the screenshot (don't use a similar font), the same yellow and the same dark box under it with a title row, three rows with white checkboxes and a "Completion - Gold 100%" row with the gold dot. My texts: title "The Last Arrest", rows "13 Years of Chaos", "Never Busted", "Made the Police Cry". Then put it on the last frame of my video with a short fade-in, and keep it inside Instagram's safe zone: nothing in the top 250 px, the bottom 420 px or the right-side icons of a 1080x1920 frame.
Música: en Instagram elige "See You Again" de la biblioteca de música, desde la parte que le quede a tu final. Es la canción de toda esta tendencia.
